01

Brand

Sane is the promise — calm, clear, in your right mind during exam week. “Sane” stands alone in the product; “Sane Notes” is the full name for stores, sign-in and documents. The mark is the Sage — the registered character (conical hat, staff, closed eyes). It is a fixed-color trademark: never recolor, stretch, flip or crop it; it sits on the look’s accent-soft tile so the tile, not the mark, changes with the theme.
Sane Notes
Study smarter.
Wordmark: “Sane” at weight 600, “Notes” at 400 in the muted ink, display face of the active look, tracking −1%. In-app chrome shows “Sane” alone. Tagline keeps the initials S·S. Minimum mark size 24 px; clear space equals the hat brim height. Source: assets/sane-sage.png (transparent), 779×776.
App icon variants
The Sage on four tile grounds — accent-soft (default), ink, paper, Cyberpunk night. The character stays identical; only the tile follows the look.
Voice
Plain, warm, specific. Sentences, not labels. "Everything you write is time-linked" beats "Audio sync enabled". Numbers are honest (5 imports a month), limits are explained, never hidden. No exclamation marks; one em dash is fine.

Library · Minimalism

Library
1 Sidebar 248 px (68 px rail under 900 px): brand, search (⌘K), 5 nav items at 42 px, Subjects with counts, plan card, Settings, current profile.
2 “Pick up where you left off” — three wide cards with a live ink thumbnail, page position and fact badges.

Editor · Paper · sidebar auto-hidden
12345
Editor
1 Opening a notebook hides the sidebar for a full-width page; the first chrome button brings it back. Then: back, title + page, undo/redo.
2 Right cluster: bookmark, audio, import PDF, paper & template, share, pages rail, focus mode (hides all chrome; a pill exits).
3 Page 800×1040 units, max 820 px wide, real vector ink; typed text is an HTML layer sized in container units. Freeform canvases are 2400×2400 and scroll both ways.
4 Pages rail 132 px with live thumbnails, bookmark tabs and “+ Page”.
5 Palette: grip · 7 tools at 44 px · 6 colors · 3 widths · page nav. Drag the grip to dock top/left/right; edge guides light up while dragging.
Search · Pop
123
Search
1 One field, 58 px, 18 px type; filters for Handwriting / Typed / PDFs / Audio.
2 “Ask my notes” answers from your own notebooks and cites the pages it used (Pro preview on Free).
3 Results are typed: handwriting shows the ink strip and its recognised text, PDFs the passage, audio the timestamp. Clicking opens the exact page.
Settings › Appearance · Bento UI
12
Settings › Appearance
1 17 look cards, each a miniature of its own tokens (ground, surface, radius, border, shadow, accent, display face). Dark mode is a separate switch so every look has a night version.
2 Wallpaper: upload (down-sampled to 1600 px, saved on device) or a preset. It renders blurred and scaled behind a tinted veil; surfaces turn 84% translucent with a 22 px backdrop blur, so ink and text keep their contrast.
Other sections: Account & plan (profiles, student status), Sync & backup, Handwriting & stylus (toolbar position, left-handed), Notifications, Privacy & export.
